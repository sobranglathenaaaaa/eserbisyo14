# Product Overview

## Product Summary

eSerbisyo is a multi-role barangay service platform that digitizes resident-facing transactions and internal barangay workflows.

The MVP focuses on replacing paper-heavy, manual processing with:

- online resident self-service
- role-based review and processing
- transparent status tracking
- digital document release
- centralized operational records

The product should feel simple for residents, efficient for staff, and accountable for barangay administrators.

## Product Goal

The goal of the MVP is to launch a usable digital service portal that enables a barangay to:

- accept and track document requests online
- review and process requests through role-based workflows
- publish announcements and service updates
- record incident and blotter reports
- collect post-transaction feedback
- maintain a resident-facing history of service activity
- provide a basic analytics view for admin users

## Success Criteria

The MVP is successful when:

- a resident can register and log in
- a resident can submit a document request
- an admin can approve or decline that request
- a staff user can process and complete that request
- the resident can see status changes and access the completed output
- admins can manage announcements and monitor service activity
- residents can submit reports and feedback without manual paper forms

## Core Product Principles

- Resident-first: flows must be understandable without training
- Transparent: every request and report should expose current status
- Accountable: key actions should be attributable to a role or user
- Practical: prioritize operational clarity over advanced automation
- Mobile-friendly: residents may primarily access the system by phone

## Final Product Direction

eSerbisyo MVP should present a complete digital barangay service journey:

- residents can request help and track progress
- staff can fulfill operational tasks
- admins can supervise, approve, and monitor activity

The experience should communicate trust, clarity, and readiness for phased expansion beyond the MVP.

## Current Implementation Directive

For the active production phase, prioritize:

- complete end-to-end feature functionality across resident, staff, and admin flows
- reliability and stable UX behavior across refresh/navigation/session states
- immediate account activation on registration with no verification dependency in UI
- essential launch security controls (auth, role checks, tenant scope, minimal rate limit)
