## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are barangay residents, barangay staff, and barangay administrators in the Philippines.
Residents need fast, low-friction access to requests, queueing, and notifications from mobile-first contexts.
Staff and admins need dense but clear operational workflows for approvals, processing, and accountability.

### Brand Personality
Trustworthy, practical, and human.
The interface should feel dependable for civic services, straightforward during stressful tasks, and respectful for mixed technical literacy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Green civic-service identity with clean, modern layouts.
Prioritize clarity and hierarchy over decorative effects in portal screens; use richer expression mainly on landing/auth surfaces.
Support both English and Filipino copy patterns with consistent terminology.

### Design Principles
1. Task-first hierarchy: always surface the next best action per role.
2. Progressive disclosure: group dense navigation and reveal complexity by section.
3. Semantic consistency: statuses, actions, and feedback use shared tokens and patterns.
4. Resilient UX: interfaces must remain usable on narrow screens, long text, and empty/error states.
5. Inclusive clarity: concise bilingual-friendly copy, clear feedback, and visible keyboard focus.
